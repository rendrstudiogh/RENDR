Rooms and prices

Six room types. One price each.

Fees are for the 9 month tenancy, per bed, as published by the university for 2026/27. You pay the hostel fee only. There is no service fee.

The hostels

Two hostels on campus, for male and female students.

Shared rooms are single gender: once the first student books a bed, the other beds in that room are offered only to students of the same gender.

Location

Where the hostels are

See Maria and Camille on Google Maps, and how far each hostel is from the buildings where lectures happen.

In every room

  • A single bed and mattress for each student
  • Air conditioning and a ceiling fan
  • A table-top fridge
  • A study desk for each student
  • Its own electricity meter. Roommates top up the credit themselves.

Who can apply, and the rules

  • Freshers, and students who stayed in the hostel before. Two years at most.
  • New students can book before admission or before getting a student ID. Create an account with your email and add your ID later.
  • One bed per student. You cannot pay for extra beds to keep a room to yourself.
  • Tenancy is 9 months, starting 1 October or 1 January.
  • Beds cannot be sold, traded or passed to another student.
  • Hostel fees are not refundable, unless you do not meet LUG entry requirements.
